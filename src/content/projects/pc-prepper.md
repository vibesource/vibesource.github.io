PC Prepper is an evolving effort to document how someone could establish comparable offline-capable AI, reference, and development tools on their own machine. A separate, private project now curates the practical reproduction guide, while the first experimental deployment and detailed operational evidence remain in a private homelab repository.

The guide is not a finished installer or a guarantee of offline readiness. This VibeSource page follows the questions, trade-offs and lessons as those capabilities are explored.
